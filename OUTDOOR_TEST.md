# 🌲 30-Minute Outdoor Field Test Protocol: Zero-Screen Dungeon Master

Follow this quick step-by-step checklist to test Zero-Screen Dungeon Master outdoors on a park trail, campus walkway, or neighborhood street.

---

## 🎒 Pre-Walk Preparation (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Charge your phone** to at least 30% battery.
- [ ] **Plug in or pair your earphones / AirPods**.
- [ ] **Open the deployed Web App URL** in Chrome or Safari on your phone.
- [ ] **(Optional) Install to Home Screen**: Tap browser options -> "Add to Home Screen" to launch in full standalone PWA mode.
- [ ] **Audio Check**: In the app, tap the sliders icon at top right -> tap **"Test Voice"** to ensure your device's Text-to-Speech is loud and clear in your earphones.

---

## 🚶 The Field Walk (20 minutes)
1. **Select an Adventure**:
   - For a park or trail: Select *"The Whispering Canopy"* (Fantasy).
   - For city streets: Select *"Signal in the Rain"* (Sci-Fi).
   - For a campus or historical district: Select *"The Forgotten Campus Bell"* (Mystery).
2. **Hit "START EXPEDITION"**:
   - Grant Geolocation permission when prompted.
   - Listen to the voice introduction.
3. **Pocket the Phone**:
   - Tap **"ENTER POCKET MODE"** (or simply tuck your phone into your pocket).
   - Start walking forward at a natural, steady pace.
4. **Encountering Checkpoints (~80 meters each)**:
   - When you have walked ~80 meters, you will feel a distinct vibration pattern in your pocket and hear the story continue in your earphones.
   - The narrator will present two paths: Option Left vs Option Right.
5. **Blind Choice Selection**:
   - Without looking at your screen, reach into your pocket and tap the **LEFT half** or **RIGHT half** of the phone screen.
   - You will hear a confirmation chime and vibration indicating your choice was registered.
   - Keep walking forward!
6. **Reach the Ending**:
   - Complete 5–7 legs of your walk (~500–600 meters total).
   - The narrator will deliver the climax resolution and victory fanfare.

---

## 🏆 Post-Walk Debrief & Media Capture (5 minutes)
- [ ] **Pull out your phone** to see your **Touch Grass Score** (aim for >90%!), total distance, steps, and calories burned.
- [ ] **Take a quick outdoor photo**: A snapshot of your trail/street and your phone displaying the Touch Grass Score badge.
- [ ] **(Optional) 15-second video clip**: A quick clip showing your earphones, the walking path, and the app narrating.
- [ ] **Tap "Share Walk Score"**: Copies your journey summary to your clipboard.
